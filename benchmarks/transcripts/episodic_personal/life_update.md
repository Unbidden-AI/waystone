# episodic_personal / life_update

Alex: Hey Riley, how's my favorite little sister doing? Still alive after the Austin move?

Riley: Alex! Hey! Yeah, barely. My back is still recovering from lifting all those boxes. But I'm officially settled in, mostly. The apartment on South Congress is great, even if it's a bit smaller than my old place in Lincoln Park.

Alex: Good to hear! I know that move was a beast. How's the Austin vibe treating you? Found your go-to coffee spot yet?

Riley: Not yet, still exploring. But the food trucks are incredible. And the weather is definitely a change from Chicago in May. Speaking of changes, what's new with you? You sounded a bit giddy when you called.

Alex: Oh, you know me too well. Big news! I got the Senior Project Manager role at Innovatech!

Riley: No way! Alex, that's amazing! Congratulations! When does it start?

Alex: Thanks! It's official starting July 1st. Comes with a pretty sweet 12% raise too, which is nice. I'm really excited about the new challenges.

Riley: That's fantastic! You totally deserve it. All those late nights you've been putting in. We definitely need to celebrate properly when I'm back for Mom and Dad's anniversary.

Alex: Absolutely! Speaking of which, Mom called me yesterday. She's got the Lakeside Country Club booked for July 15th. She wants us to coordinate the photo slideshow.

Riley: Ugh, the slideshow. Always our job, isn't it? I'll start digging through the old photo albums when I'm home. Do you remember where that box of childhood photos is? The one with all the embarrassing haircuts?

Alex: Probably in the attic, next to Dad's old golf clubs. I'll try to get a head start on digitizing some of the older ones. So, what have you been up to besides unpacking? Any new Austin adventures?

Riley: Well, I actually signed up for a beginner's pottery class at The Clay Studio downtown. It's been surprisingly therapeutic. My first mug looks like a lopsided potato, but it's *my* lopsided potato.

Alex: Pottery? That's so cool, Riley! I can totally see you getting into that. Send me a picture of the potato mug!

Riley: I will! It's a work in progress. But honestly, it's been a good distraction from... well, some other stuff.

Alex: Oh? What kind of other stuff? Spill.

Riley: It's Sarah. Remember Sarah, my old roommate from UIUC?

Alex: Yeah, of course. What about her? You guys still talk?

Riley: Not really, which is part of the problem. She's been really weird since I moved out. She sent me a Venmo request last week for $45, claiming I owe her for the last month's internet bill.

Alex: $45? But you moved out at the end of May, right? Like, May 28th?

Riley: Exactly! And I paid my full share for May before I left. I even have the bank statement showing the transfer to her on May 25th. She's saying I should pay for June because the bill comes out at the beginning of the month.

Alex: That's ridiculous. You weren't even living there in June. Did you try talking to her?

Riley: I texted her, explaining that I paid my share up to my departure date and that I'm not responsible for June. She just replied with "It's common courtesy, Riley." Like, what?

Alex: "Common courtesy" to pay for a service you're not using? That's not courtesy, that's just trying to get money out of you. Did you block the Venmo request?

Riley: Not yet, I just ignored it. I don't want to cause a huge fight, but it's so frustrating. We were good friends for years.

Alex: You don't owe her that money, Riley. You paid your dues. If she pushes it, just send her a screenshot of your May payment and tell her that's all you're paying. Don't let her guilt-trip you.

Riley: You're right. It's just annoying to have this hanging over my head when I'm trying to get settled here.

Alex: I get it. But stand your ground. You've got enough on your plate. Anyway, I gotta jump on a call in a few minutes. But seriously, congrats on the pottery, and don't let Sarah get to you.

Riley: Thanks, Alex. And seriously, huge congrats on the promotion! We'll talk soon about the slideshow.

Alex: Sounds good, sis. Love you!

Riley: Love you too! Bye!
