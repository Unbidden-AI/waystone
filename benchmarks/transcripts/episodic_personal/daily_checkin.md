# episodic_personal / daily_checkin

Casey: Ugh, what a week. This pasta is exactly what I needed. How was your day, Drew?

Drew: Hey, Case. Yeah, this smells incredible. My day was... sweaty. Just got back from the gym. How was your shift at St. Jude's? You looked wiped when you got home.

Casey: Wiped is an understatement. We're still dealing with the fallout from the new EHR system migration. It's been a nightmare.

Drew: Oh no, still? I thought they were supposed to have ironed out the kinks by now.

Casey: You'd think, right? Yesterday, we had a critical incident report because a medication order for Mrs. Henderson on 3B got completely lost in the system. Luckily, Nurse Miller caught it before her 10 AM dose was missed, but it was a close call.

Drew: That's terrifying. Patient safety is no joke. What a mess.

Casey: Exactly. I spent half my shift documenting the incident and then training three new hires on the "workarounds" for the system's glitches. My brain is fried.

Drew: I can only imagine. Well, at least you're home now. Speaking of sweating, my new fitness routine is kicking my butt.

Casey: Oh yeah, the CrossFit intro, right? How's 'Ironclad Gym' on Elm Street treating you?

Drew: Brutally. Coach Mike had us doing burpees and box jumps for what felt like an hour on Wednesday. My quads are still screaming. I can barely walk up the stairs to apartment 204.

Casey: Sounds intense! But good for you for sticking with it. I need to get back into some kind of routine myself.

Drew: You should! It's tough, but I already feel stronger. Hey, did you hear Mr. Henderson's dog again last night? From apartment 302?

Casey: Ugh, yes! It was barking non-stop from 11 PM until almost 1 AM. I swear, I was about to go up there myself.

Drew: Right? I'm seriously considering leaving a polite but firm note on their door tomorrow morning. My sleep schedule is already messed up from early gym sessions.

Casey: You absolutely should. It's been happening too often lately. Anyway, shifting gears to something more fun – are we still on for that Oregon Coast road trip next month?

Drew: Oh, definitely! I've been looking forward to it. What dates were we thinking again?

Casey: I was thinking May 17th to 19th. That's a Friday to Sunday, so we'd only need to take one day off work.

Drew: Perfect! I can definitely swing that. Have you thought about where we'd stay? I saw a cute Airbnb in Cannon Beach near Haystack Rock.

Casey: That's exactly what I was looking at! It had a little balcony, right? Let's book it this weekend before it gets snatched up.

Drew: Deal. I'll send you the link later tonight. So, anything else exciting happen in your world this week, besides the EHR drama?

Casey: Not really, just work and trying to keep my plants alive. What about you? Any other big news?

Drew: Well... there is one thing.

Casey: Oh? Spill! You're making that face.

Drew: I, uh, I ran into Sarah.

Casey: Sarah? Your ex-girlfriend Sarah? From college? No way!

Drew: The one and only. She texted me out of the blue on Tuesday. We grabbed coffee at 'The Daily Grind' today.

Casey: Wow! How was it? I haven't heard you mention her in ages.

Drew: It was... really good, actually. Like, surprisingly good. We talked for almost three hours. It felt like old times, but... different, in a good way.

Casey: So, are we talking rekindled romance here, Drew? Don't leave me hanging!

Drew: Maybe? We're getting dinner on Friday night at that new Italian place, 'Trattoria del Sole.'

Casey: That's huge! I'm so happy for you, man. I always thought you two had something special.

Drew: Yeah, I guess we'll see. It's a bit nerve-wracking, but exciting too.

Casey: Well, I'm rooting for you! This is way more interesting than my EHR woes.

Drew: Thanks, Case. And hey, this pasta is seriously amazing. You outdid yourself.

Casey: Glad you like it! More wine?
