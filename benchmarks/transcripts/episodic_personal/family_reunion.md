# episodic_personal / family_reunion

Maya: Hey! Is that really you, Jordan? It's been ages!

Jordan: Maya! Oh my god, it's so good to hear your voice! It feels like forever, hasn't it? Like six months at least.

Maya: At least! So, spill! How's the wedding planning going with Sam? Are you pulling your hair out yet?

Jordan: Honestly, it's a whirlwind, but mostly in a good way. We finally locked down The Old Mill Estate for October 12th. Can you believe it?

Maya: October 12th! That's perfect, fall colors will be gorgeous. How many guests are you thinking?

Jordan: Around 180. Sam's actually been amazing, surprisingly involved. He even picked out the band, 'The Groove Collective,' and they're fantastic.

Maya: That's fantastic! I'm so happy for you both. And how about you? How's the new gig at Synapse AI? I saw your LinkedIn update.

Jordan: Oh, it's been intense but incredible. I'm a Senior Product Manager there now, focusing on their enterprise solutions. We just closed our Series B funding round last month, so things are moving fast.

Maya: Wow, Synapse AI! That's huge. Are you still working on those predictive analytics models you mentioned?

Jordan: Exactly! It's challenging, but I'm learning so much about scaling ML operations and integrating AI into existing workflows. The hours are long, though, especially with the new product launch slated for Q4.

Maya: I can imagine. Speaking of long hours and stress, my mom had a bit of a scare back in late April.

Jordan: Oh no, Maya! What happened? I'm so sorry, I didn't know.

Maya: It was acute appendicitis. She needed emergency surgery. It was really touch and go for a few days, and I was so worried.

Jordan: That's terrifying. Is she okay now? Is she fully recovered?

Maya: Thankfully, yes. She's made a full recovery, but it really put things into perspective. I was commuting to her place in Sacramento every weekend for a month to help out.

Jordan: I'm so relieved to hear she's better. That must have been incredibly stressful for you on top of the new job.

Maya: It was. But hey, enough about that. You mentioned a trip to Portugal on Instagram? Your photos looked absolutely stunning!

Jordan: Oh, yes! Sam and I went for two weeks in late May. We started in Lisbon, then took a train to Porto, and finished with a few days relaxing in the Algarve. It was exactly what we needed.

Maya: That sounds absolutely amazing! I remember us dreaming about backpacking Europe after Professor Davies's history class in sophomore year.

Jordan: We did! We were so convinced we'd go right after graduation in 2018. Instead, we just ate pizza and watched bad movies in our dorm for a week.

Maya: And complained about the terrible cafeteria food! Good times, though. I miss those days sometimes, just being carefree.

Jordan: Me too. Life's definitely gotten more complicated since then. But in a good way, mostly.

Maya: Definitely. So, when are we actually going to see each other in person? I need a proper hug and a real catch-up.

Jordan: Soon! How about next Saturday for brunch? I know a great spot downtown, 'The Daily Grind,' that just opened.

Maya: Perfect! Text me the details. I'd love to catch up properly.

Jordan: Will do! So good to talk, Maya.

Maya: You too, Jordan! Talk soon.
