# meeting_notes / executive_review

Elena: Good morning, everyone. Let's kick off this Q3 review. David, can you walk us through the numbers?

David: Absolutely, Elena. Q3 closed at $18 million in revenue, which is unfortunately $2 million short of our $20 million target. Our ARR growth slowed to 12% this quarter, down from 15% in Q2.

Elena: That's a significant miss. Marco, what happened on the sales front? Were there specific deals that slipped?

Marco: Yes, Elena. We had two major enterprise deals, Project Phoenix and the Delta Solutions contract, that were expected to close in September but pushed to early Q4. Both are still very much alive, but the sales cycle extended due to some late-stage security reviews.

David: Those two deals alone would have brought us much closer to target. The impact on our cash flow and burn rate is noticeable.

Elena: Understood. Given this, we need to make some tough decisions on cost control. Effective immediately, we will be implementing a 20% reduction in our contractor headcount across the entire company. David, I need you to oversee this process to ensure it's executed by October 31st.

Keisha: Elena, how will that impact our engineering projects? We rely heavily on specialized contractors for some of our core development.

Elena: It will require careful planning, Keisha. We need to prioritize ruthlessly. This isn't just about cost; it's about focusing our internal resources on the most critical path items.

Marco: Will this affect our ability to scale up for new customer implementations in Q4?

Elena: That's precisely what we need to manage. Which brings me to my next point. Keisha, I'm seeing some red flags on engineering velocity, especially with the new platform features. Our sprint commitments have been consistently under-delivered.

Keisha: I acknowledge that, Elena. We've been grappling with some technical debt on the Atlas project, and resource allocation has been stretched thin across too many initiatives. We also had a few key senior engineers out on leave.

Elena: And the Acme Corp onboarding. It's been stalled for three weeks now. Marco, you're feeling the heat on that, right?

Marco: Absolutely, Elena. Acme is a critical Q4 ARR driver, and their frustration is growing. They're expecting to go live by November 15th.

Keisha: The Acme blocker is a complex API integration issue with their legacy system. Our team is swamped, and we're short on senior backend engineers who understand their specific architecture. We've assigned Sarah Chen to lead it, but it's proving more challenging than anticipated.

Elena: We need a clear path to resolution there, Keisha. This can't drag on.

David: Every day Acme isn't live, it impacts our recognized revenue for Q4.

Elena: Right. Let's shift to Q4. Marco, what's the pipeline looking like? Can we make up for the Q3 miss?

Marco: The Q4 pipeline is robust. We have GlobalTech and Innovate Solutions, the two deals that slipped, plus a new prospect, Zenith Systems, all looking strong. Combined, they represent over $7 million in potential ARR. We're pushing hard for early closes.

David: Pipeline health is good, but conversion rates need to improve significantly to hit our revised Q4 target of $22 million.

Elena: Agreed. Let's talk action items. Marco, I need a detailed plan for accelerating those Q4 deals, especially GlobalTech and Zenith. And a weekly update on Acme Corp's progress, directly to me.

Marco: Understood, Elena. I'll have that plan by EOD Friday.

Elena: Keisha, for you: a concrete plan to resolve the Acme Corp integration blocker within the next two weeks. I need a dedicated tiger team on it. And a strategy for improving overall engineering velocity, considering the contractor reduction.

Keisha: I'll prioritize Acme immediately. For velocity, we'll re-evaluate sprint commitments, focus on critical path items, and look at internal cross-training. I'll have a proposal by end of week.

Elena: David, you'll work with all VPs to ensure the 20% contractor reduction is implemented smoothly and efficiently by October 31st, with minimal disruption to critical projects.

David: Confirmed. I'll circulate the guidelines and schedule individual meetings with each department head.

Elena: Good. We missed Q3, but Q4 is still within reach if we execute flawlessly on these items. Let's regroup next week for a quick check-in on Acme and the contractor plan. Thanks, everyone.

Marco: Thanks, Elena.

Keisha: Thank you.

David: Appreciate it.
